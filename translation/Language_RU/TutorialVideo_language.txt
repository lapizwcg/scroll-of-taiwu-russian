PartsDesc_0_0
Left-click a tile to select it, then click the same tile again to move there. (Each move consumes <color=#orange>Energy</color>.)
PartsDesc_0_1
You can also move using the <color=#orange>WASD</color> keys on your keyboard. (Each move consumes <color=#orange>Energy</color>.)
PartsDesc_1_0
When your inventory is overloaded, the <color=#orange>Energy</color> cost of tile movement will increase significantly. A warning will be displayed at the top of the screen.\nEquip a <color=#orange>Vehicle</color> to increase your character's <color=#orange>Travel Bag Weight Limit</color>.
PartsDesc_1_1
Click the character portrait in the lower-left corner to view your character and enter the Character Information Interface.
PartsDesc_1_2
Hover over the Equipment tab and switch to the Mounts interface.
PartsDesc_1_3
Click the <color=#orange>Mounts</color> tab and equip the Huanxin's Thin Horse.
PartsDesc_2_0
Vehicles not only increase Travel Bag Weight Limit, but also reduce the <color=#orange>Energy</color> consumed while traveling.
PartsDesc_2_1
Left-click a tile to view the amount of <color=#orange>Energy</color> required to move there.
PartsDesc_2_2
In addition to traveling, most character actions also consume <color=#orange>Energy</color>.\nYou can check your remaining <color=#orange>Energy</color> for the current month at the bottom center of the screen.
PartsDesc_3_0
When remaining Energy is insufficient, you need to perform a <color=#orange>Month Advance</color> to recover Energy.\nClick the <color=#orange>Energy Bar</color> at the bottom center of the screen, or press the space bar, to perform a Month Advance.
PartsDesc_4_0
While on a Settlement tile, click Industry above the tile to enter the Industry View.
PartsDesc_5_0
Click Construction Space in the lower-right corner of the Industry View to enter the Building Overview screen.
PartsDesc_5_1
Select the building you wish to construct and assign workers to it.
PartsDesc_5_2
Place the building on a suitable Industry Zone tile to begin construction.
PartsDesc_6_0
Construction takes time to complete. Perform <color=#orange>Month Advance</color> and wait for construction to finish.
PartsDesc_7_0
When standing on a tile containing an <color=#orange>Encounter</color>, click the Encounter icon to enter it.
PartsDesc_8_0
During battle, hold the right mouse button in front of or behind your character to move forward or backward, changing the distance between you and your opponent.\nAttacks can only hit enemies within your <color=#orange>Attack Range</color>.
PartsDesc_8_1
You can also move using the <color=#orange>AD</color> keys on your keyboard.
PartsDesc_9_0
In battle, click the left mouse button or use the <color=#orange>J</color> key on the keyboard to perform a Weapon Attack.
PartsDesc_10_0
Each weapon has specific <color=#orange>Techniques</color>. When performing a Weapon Attack, attacks are made in the order of these Techniques.
PartsDesc_10_1
Different Moves correspond to different Hit Types. Hit Types are divided into three types: <color=#orange>Force</color>, <color=#orange>Dexterity</color>, and <color=#orange>Accuracy</color>.
PartsDesc_11_0
Accumulated damage generates <color=#orange>Defeat Markers</color>, which are displayed in the Defeat Marker Gauge at the top of the screen.\nWhen a character's <color=#orange>Defeat Markers</color> reach the defeat threshold, that character is defeated.
PartsDesc_12_0
Double-click an Encounter tile, or use the <color=#orange>WASD</color> keys to move.
PartsDesc_12_1
When moving in an Encounter, other characters within the Encounter may also move or perform various actions.
PartsDesc_13_0
Tiles contain various types of <color=#orange>Resources</color>. Different terrain types provide different kinds and quantities of resources.\nGathering in terrain rich in <color=#orange>Wood</color> will yield more lumber.
PartsDesc_14_0
Click the Tile Interaction button at the bottom of the screen and select the resource you wish to gather.
PartsDesc_14_1
You can also right-click a tile to quickly open the Tile Interaction interface.
PartsDesc_15_0
As characters accumulate insights through daily cultivation and adventure, they will learn various <color=#orange>Aspiration Skills</color>.\nClick the Aspiration button at the bottom of the screen to enter the Aspiration interface.
PartsDesc_15_1
Unlocked Aspiration Skills will be highlighted. Click a skill to equip it. Only equipped skills can take effect or be used.
PartsDesc_16_0
Hover over the Aspiration button at the bottom of the screen to view currently equipped Aspiration Skills in the expanded window.
PartsDesc_16_1
Icons of actively usable skills will light up. Click a skill icon to use that skill.
PartsDesc_37_0
Click on the tile where Bamboo Hut is on the map to quickly find your way back to Bamboo Hut.
PartsDesc_17_0
Click Industry above the tile to enter the Industry View.\nIn the Industry View, click the Bamboo Cut building to enter its details screen.
PartsDesc_17_1
Click Carpentry to enter the Crafting interface.
PartsDesc_18_0
Click Target Product in the center of the Crafting interface, switch to <color=#orange>Specified Target Product</color>, and select the item you wish to craft.
PartsDesc_18_1
Switch the Reagent panel on the left to <color=#orange>Private Vault</color> and select the <color=#orange>Reagent</color> to be used in crafting.
PartsDesc_18_2
Switch the Tool panel on the right to <color=#orange>Private Vault</color> and select an appropriate <color=#orange>Tool</color>.
PartsDesc_18_3
Invest the required <color=#orange>Resources</color> for crafting. Use the <color=#orange>+</color> and <color=#orange>-</color> buttons to adjust resource quantities.
PartsDesc_18_4
Once all crafting requirements are met, click the Start Crafting button to complete the process.
PartsDesc_19_0
Casting a <color=#orange>Annihilation Martial Arts</color> usually consumes <color=#orange>Inhale and Stance</color>.
PartsDesc_19_1
<color=#orange>Inhale</color> naturally regenerates over time during battle.
PartsDesc_19_2
Landing weapon attacks on enemies or being struck by enemy weapon attacks will accumulate <color=#orange>Stance</color>.
PartsDesc_20_0
Casting a <color=#orange>Annihilation Martial Arts</color> requires <color=#orange>Style</color>. Style is gained through weapon attacks.\nAfter successfully hitting an enemy with a weapon attack, you will gain a corresponding <color=#orange>technique</color> based on the <color=#orange>style</color> used.
PartsDesc_21_0
Hover the mouse over a Martial Art icon to view its <color=#orange>usage requirements</color> and effects.
PartsDesc_21_1
After meeting the Martial Art's <color=#orange>usage requirements</color>, click its icon to cast it.
PartsDesc_22_0
Using <color=#orange>Annihilation Martial Arts</color> to attack is the primary way to deal damage in battle.\nThe higher the displayed <color=#orange>Power Multiplier</color>, the greater the damage dealt by the Annihilation Attack.
PartsDesc_23_0
Click Study Books to the right of the Energy bar to enter the Book Study interface.
PartsDesc_23_1
Select a book to study to complete the preparation process.
PartsDesc_24_0
During Month Advance, one study session is automatically performed.
PartsDesc_25_0
While studying, you may trigger an <color=#orange>Epiphany</color>. Corresponding visual effects will appear in the Study Book slot.
PartsDesc_25_1
Click the Study button to enter the Study interface, then click the Epiphany button to enter the Epiphany interface.
PartsDesc_26_0
In the Epiphany interface, you can consume the character's <color=#orange>Comprehension</color> to use <color=#orange>Study Strategies</color>, increasing the book's Study Efficiency or Study Progress.\nSelect any <color=#orange>Chapter</color> and choose a Study Strategy to apply it to that Chapter.
PartsDesc_27_0
Click the lower-left corner to view the character and enter the Breakthrough interface.
PartsDesc_27_1
Select fully studied Martial Arts, then choose 1 <color=#orange>General Outline</color> and 5 <color=#orange>Mind Method</color> to begin the breakthrough.
PartsDesc_28_0
Start from any <color=#orange>Starting Node</color> in the four corners of the Breakthrough interface, successively connect adjacent Breakthrough Nodes until you connect the central <color=#orange>Goal Node</color>, and Breakthrough is complete.
PartsDesc_29_0
Click Microcosmic Circulation to the left of the Energy bar to enter the Microcosmic Circulation interface. Select a Qi Art that you have learned, and begin Microcosmic Circulation.
PartsDesc_30_0
During Month Advance, one Microcosmic Circulation is automatically performed.
PartsDesc_31_0
Through Microcosmic Circulation, you can obtain <color=#orange>Qi</color> and <color=#orange>Circulation True Qi</color>, improving your internal cultivation. The effects of Microcosmic Circulation can be viewed in the Microcosmic Circulation interface.
PartsDesc_32_0
Click the lower-left corner to view the character and enter the Qi interface.
PartsDesc_32_1
Click <color=#orange>+</color> to condense Qi into various types of <color=#orange>True Qi</color>, each enhancing different combat attributes. Click <color=#orange>-</color> to convert True Qi back into Qi.
PartsDesc_33_0
Open the Character interface to view the character's Traits. Traits affect various character attributes, and some may also grant unique effects.
PartsDesc_34_0
Open the Character interface and enter the Qi tab to view the Five Element attributes of the character's Qi. Qi attributes influence the power of Martial Arts associated with their respective elements.
PartsDesc_34_1
When two mutually opposing Five Element Qi become dominant, <color=#orange>Qi Conflict</color> will occur. Casting Martial Arts of the corresponding elements while in this state will cause the character to suffer <color=#orange>Recoil</color>.
PartsDesc_35_0
Open the Character interface and enter the Qi tab to view the character's <color=#orange>Pure Essence</color>. The Purity Realm reflects a character's battle strength to a certain extent. When the Pure Essence difference between two characters is too great, a powerful Pure Essence Suppression effect will occur.
PartsDesc_36_0
Move to the tile where the <color=#orange>Event</color> is located, and click the Event icon to enter the Event.
PartsDesc_36_1
In the Event interface, after a dialogue segment ends, click the next <color=#orange>Event Node</color> to advance the event.
