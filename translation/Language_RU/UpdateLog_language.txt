SubentryDescriptions_5_0
Compound toxins is a novel system blending six base toxins: During combat, if a character is afflicted with at least three types of poison, the activation of any poison can trigger special compound poison effects. There are up to 20 unique effects possible from all combinations of the six toxins, and multiple effects can trigger simultaneously. Outside of combat, characters can apply up to three different toxins to a single item, creating up to 20 different possible poison effects based on these combinations.
SubentryDescriptions_5_1
In Cricket Duels, adjusted the quantity and tier of resources and items bet by opponents, while increasing the resources characters hold. This allows characters who meet the conditions to bet with the highest quantities or tier of resources and items available.
SubentryDescriptions_5_2
Added damage thresholds for a character's Psyche, Wound, and seven specific body parts. Each body part has separate thresholds for external and internal injuries. When damage reaches these thresholds, a visible Injury Marker will appear to clearly indicate the wound status. Also tweaked some Martial Art effects, improved the parry system, and adjusted how openings affect final damage. Significantly boosted the accuracy of pinpoint strike attacks and introduced a new adaptive weapon type: the Larynxound. A complete auto-battle settings panel has been implemented, along with overall AI improvements.
